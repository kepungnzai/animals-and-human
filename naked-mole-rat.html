Meet the Naked Mole-Rat

It’s not a mole and it’s not a rat — it’s its very own awesome kind of rodent!

🌎

Home: East Africa

It digs long tunnel cities under the dry grasslands of Kenya, Ethiopia and Somalia. It barely ever comes to the surface!

📏

Tiny but Tough

Only about 8–10 cm long — a bit bigger than a crayon — with pink, wrinkly, nearly hairless skin. That skin earned it the funny name “naked”!

🦷️

Super Teeth!

Two big buck teeth dig tunnels, and a mole-rat can move them separately — like tiny front shovels. Its lips close behind its teeth so it never swallows dirt!

🐘

“Queen” Rules

Like bees, a colony has one special queen, a king, and workers. The queen is the only one who has babies — up to 27 at a time!

Colonies can grow to about 300 members — all digging, cuddling and even sharing food like one big family. Cuteness level: debatable. Coolness level: off the charts! 😍
